## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases:

- 做重阳糕 (zuò Chóngyáng gāo) – make Chongyang cake
- 拿糯米粉 (ná nuòmǐ fěn) – take glutinous rice flour
- 加水 (jiā shuǐ) – add water
- 用筷子搅拌 (yòng kuàizi jiǎobàn) – stir with chopsticks
- 切碎红枣和核桃 (qiē suì hóngzǎo hé hétáo) – chop up red dates and walnuts
- 放糖 (fàng táng) – add sugar
- 压平 (yā píng) – press flat
- 送给邻居 (sòng gěi línjū) – give to the neighbor as a gift

### Resultative Complements:

- 切碎 (qiē suì) – cut into pieces (切 + 碎 “cut” + “broken into pieces”)
- 压平 (yā píng) – press flat (压 + 平 “press” + “flat”)
- 拿下来 (ná xiàlái) – take down/off (拿 + 下来 “take” + “down”)
- 打开门 (dǎkāi mén) – open the door (打 + 开 “hit” + “open”, here meaning to open)

### Sequential Actions with 先…然后…再…:

- 先拿出糯米粉，放在一个大碗里。
- 然后……用筷子搅拌。
- 再放一些糖。

### Expressing Duration with 过了 + time:

- 过了二十分钟 (guò le èrshí fēnzhōng) – after twenty minutes passed

### Expressing “a little bit” with 一点点 and 有点:

- 一点点加 (yīdiǎndiǎn jiā) – add a little at a time
- 手有点累 (shǒu yǒudiǎn lèi) – hand is a little tired
- 不要太甜，对身体不好 (búyào tài tián, duì shēntǐ bù hǎo) – don’t make it too sweet, not good for health

### The 把 Sentence Structure:

- 把糯米粉放在一个大碗里 (bǎ nuòmǐ fěn fàng zài yī gè dà wǎn lǐ) – put the glutinous rice flour into a big bowl

- 把米粉放进小蒸笼里 (bǎ mǐfěn fàng jìn xiǎo zhēnglóng lǐ) – put the rice flour into the small steamer
- 把蒸笼放在锅上 (bǎ zhēnglóng fàng zài guō shàng) – put the steamer on the pot
- 把重阳糕放在盘子里 (bǎ Chóngyáng gāo fàng zài pánzi lǐ) – put the Chongyang cake on the plate
