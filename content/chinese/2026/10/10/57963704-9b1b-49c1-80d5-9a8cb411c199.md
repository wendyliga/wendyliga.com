## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases

- 准备礼物 (zhǔnbèi lǐwù) — prepare gifts
- 买礼物 (mǎi lǐwù) — buy gifts
- 表演朗诵 (biǎoyǎn lǎngsòng) — perform a recitation
- 倒茶 (dào chá) — pour tea
- 切糕 (qiē gāo) — cut cake
- 品尝重阳糕 (pǐncháng Chóngyáng gāo) — taste Chongyang cake

### Resultative and Degree Complements

- 放在桌子上 (fàng zài zhuōzi shàng) — put on the table
- 朗诵完了 (lǎngsòng wán le) — finished reciting
- 读得很认真 (dú de hěn rènzhēn) — read very carefully
- 读得真好 (dú de zhēn hǎo) — read really well

### Useful Sentence Patterns

- 快 + verb + 了：快到了 (kuài dào le) — it is almost here; something is about to happen
- 为 + person + verb：为爷爷奶奶准备礼物 (wèi yéye nǎinai zhǔnbèi lǐwù) — prepare gifts for grandparents
- 给 + person + verb + object：给爷爷倒了一杯茶 (gěi yéye dào le yì bēi chá) — pour grandpa a cup of tea
- 把 + object + verb + complement：把礼物放在桌子上 (bǎ lǐwù fàng zài zhuōzi shàng) — put the gifts on the table
- 虽然……但是……：虽然他们有一点紧张，但是读得很认真 (suīrán tāmen yǒu yìdiǎn jǐnzhāng, dànshì dú de hěn rènzhēn) — although they were a little nervous, they read very carefully
- 一边……一边……：一边听一边笑 (yìbiān tīng yìbiān xiào) — listen and laugh at the same time
- 真 + adjective：真懂事 (zhēn dǒngshì) — really thoughtful; 真感动 (zhēn gǎndòng) — really moved
