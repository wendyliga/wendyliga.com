## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### The 快……了 Structure

- 重阳节快到了。
- This structure shows that something is about to happen.
- Pattern: 快 + verb/adjective + 了

### The 一边……一边…… Structure

- 他们一边走，一边看花。
- This pattern shows that two actions happen at the same time.

### Resultative Complements

- 坐下来：The complement 下来 shows the direction or result of sitting down.
- 戴上：The complement 上 shows the result of putting the flower on.

### The 给 + Person + Verb Phrase Structure

- 丽丽给爷爷戴上一朵小菊花。
- Here, “给爷爷” means “for Grandpa.”

### Adverbial 地

- 轻轻地给爷爷戴上。
- The adverb 轻轻地 modifies the verb to show how the action was done.

### Verb-Object Phrases

- 爬山
- 喝菊花茶
- 吃重阳糕
- 拍照片
- 倒茶
- 戴花
