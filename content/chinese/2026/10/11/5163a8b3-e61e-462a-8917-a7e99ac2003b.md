## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### 1. Expressing Dates
- 十月十三日是重阳节。
- In Chinese, dates go from larger units to smaller units: month first, then day, then the verb.

### 2. Resultative Complements
- 走累了 — “walked and became tired”
- 走到了 — “walked and arrived”
- A verb plus a result word shows the result of the action.

### 3. Verb-Object Phrases
- 爬山 — “climb a mountain”
- 讲故事 — “tell a story”
- 登高 — “climb high”
- 吃糕 — “eat cake”

### 4. Simultaneous Actions with 一边……一边……
- 他们一边吃糕，一边看山。
- This pattern connects two actions happening at the same time.

### 5. Using 就 to Link Condition and Result
- 好吃就多吃一块。
- 就 connects the condition “好吃” with the result “多吃一块”.
