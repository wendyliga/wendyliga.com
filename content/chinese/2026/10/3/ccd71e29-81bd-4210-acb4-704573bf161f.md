## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases:

- **看花展** (kàn huāzhǎn) – to see a flower exhibition  
- **拍照片** (pāi zhàopiàn) – to take photos  
- **做相册** (zuò xiàngcè) – to make an album  
- **写一句话** (xiě yí jù huà) – to write one sentence  
- **选照片** (xuǎn zhàopiàn) – to select photos  

### Resultative Complements:

- **多极了** (duō jí le) – extremely many (used after adjectives to show a high degree)  
- **拍下来** (pāi xiàlái) – to capture (by taking a photo), where 下来 indicates a result of the action  
- **商量好了** (shāngliang hǎo le) – to have finished discussing, where 好 indicates completion of the action  

### Sequential Actions with 先……再……:

- **先选爷爷和菊花的合照，再选一些最美的菊花照片** (xiān xuǎn yéye hé júhuā de hézhào, zài xuǎn yìxiē zuì měi de júhuā zhàopiàn) – first select the photos of Grandpa with the chrysanthemums, then select some of the most beautiful chrysanthemum photos. This structure shows the order of actions.

### Expressing Future Intention with 要:

- **我要多拍几张照片** (wǒ yào duō pāi jǐ zhāng zhàopiàn) – I want to take a few more photos. The word 要 expresses an intention or plan for the near future.

### The Particle 了 for Change of State:

- **今天爷爷很开心** (jīntiān yéye hěn kāixīn) – Grandpa is very happy today. Here 了 is implied in the context of a completed or newly realized state, and the sentence structure follows the pattern of “今天 + subject + 很 + adjective.”

### The Conjunction 不仅……还……:

- **他们不仅看了美丽的花展，还留下了很多美好的回忆** (tāmen bùjǐn kàn le měilì de huāzhǎn, hái liúxià le hěn duō měihǎo de huíyì) – They not only saw a beautiful flower exhibition but also left many wonderful memories. This pattern expresses “not only… but also…”
