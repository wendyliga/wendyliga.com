## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases

- 做月饼 — make mooncakes  
- 做兔子灯笼 — make a rabbit lantern  
- 讲故事 — tell a story  
- 看月亮 — watch the moon  
- 吃月饼 — eat mooncakes  
- 想念家人 — miss one’s family  

### Resultative and Directional Complements

- 拿出 — take out  
  妈妈拿出面粉、糖和鸡蛋。  
- 得到 — get, receive  
  他得到了一颗特别的药。  
- 飞到 — fly to  
  她飞到了月亮上。  
- 坐在 — sit at  
  一家人坐在外面。  

### The Structure 又……又……

- 月亮又大又圆。  
  The moon is both big and round.  
  This structure is used to describe two qualities at the same time.

### The Structure 因为……所以……

- 她一定很想家，所以我们一家人要在一起。  
  She must miss home very much, so our family should stay together.  
  This structure expresses a reason and a result.

### Complements with 得

- 人们生活得很辛苦。  
- 这个中秋节，小明和丽丽过得非常开心。  
  Here, 得 introduces a description of how an action is done.
