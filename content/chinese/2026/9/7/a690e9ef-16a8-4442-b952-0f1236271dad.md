## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases:

- **做卡片 ** — "to make a card." The action verb 做 takes 卡片 as its object.
- **摘花 ** — "to pick flowers." The same verb–object pattern with a different verb.
- **送老师一份礼物 ** — "to give the teacher a present." 送 can take two objects: the recipient (老师) comes first, followed by the thing given (礼物).

### Resultative Complements:

- **做好 ** — 好 placed after the verb shows that the action was completed successfully: 晚上十点，他终于把卡片做好了。
- **带到 ** — 到 placed after the verb shows that the object reached a destination: 她小心地把花带到了学校。

### The 把 Structure:

- 他们把礼物送给了老师。把 moves the object in front of the verb to emphasize what happens to it.
- Pattern: Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + Complement + 了.

### Verb + 得:

- 虽然他写得有点慢，但是写得非常仔细。得 connects the verb to a description of how the action is done.

### 越来越:

- 看到你们越来越努力。越来越 + adjective means "more and more…"
