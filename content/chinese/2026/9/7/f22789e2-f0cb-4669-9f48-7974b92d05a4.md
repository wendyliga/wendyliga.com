## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### The “快……了” Structure (About to Happen):

- 教师节快到了。— “Teachers' Day is coming soon.”
- 快 + verb phrase + 了 expresses that something is about to happen in the near future.

### Verb-Object Phrases:

- 写汉字 (xiě Hànzì) — "to write Chinese characters"; the verb 写 takes 汉字 as its object.
- 上课 (shàng kè) — "to have class"; the verb 上 takes 课 as its object.

### Resultative Complements:

- 写好了 (xiě hǎo le) — the complement 好 after 写 shows the action was completed successfully: "wrote (and finished)."
- 来到 (lái dào) — the complement 到 after 来 shows arrival at a place: "came to (a place)."

### The “有点儿 + Adjective” Pattern:

- 小明有点儿紧张。— "Xiaoming is a little nervous."
- 有点儿 is placed before an adjective and is usually used for situations or feelings you do not want.

### Degree Complement “极了”:

- 小明高兴极了。— "Xiaoming is extremely happy."
- Adjective + 极了 expresses a very high degree of a quality.

### The “让” Causative Structure:

- 让老师天天都高兴。— "make the teacher happy every day."
- 让 + person + verb/adjective means one person or thing causes another to do or feel something.
