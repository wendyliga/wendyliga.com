## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases:

- 摘花 (zhāi huā) — "to pick flowers": 路边有很多野花，我们摘一些吧。
- 做卡片 (zuò kǎpiàn) — "to make a card": 我还要做一张卡片，写上"老师谢谢您"。
- 送礼物 (sòng lǐwù) — "to give a present": 我想送老师一份礼物。

### The 把 (bǎ) Sentence:

- 把野花和卡片一起送给了老师 — The 把 structure moves the object in front of the verb to show what happens to it. Pattern: Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + other elements.

### 一边……一边…… (yìbiān...yìbiān...):

- 他们一边走，一边摘花。 — Used when one person does two actions at the same time.

### 越……越…… (yuè...yuè...):

- 一定会写得越来越好。 — Shows that something keeps improving with each try.

### 又……又…… (yòu...yòu...):

- 这次写得又整齐又漂亮。 — Connects two qualities of the same thing.

### Resultative Complements:

- 写上 (xiě shang) — "to write onto" (上 shows the writing stays on the card): 还写上了"教师节快乐"。
- 写完 (xiě wán) — "to finish writing" (完 means the action is completed): 写完以后，他看了看。
- 收到 (shōu dào) — "to receive" (到 shows the result has arrived): 这是我收到的最好的礼物。

### Verb Reduplication:

- 丽丽想了想 (xiǎng le xiǎng) — "thought for a moment"; 他看了看 (kàn le kàn) — "took a look". Repeating the verb makes the action feel short and light.
