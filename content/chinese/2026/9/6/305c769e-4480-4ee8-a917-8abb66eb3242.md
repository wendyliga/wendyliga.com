## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases

- **摘花 (zhāi huā)** – "to pick flowers." The verb 摘 takes 花 as its object. Example from the story: 丽丽摘了很多漂亮的花。
- **做卡片 (zuò kǎpiàn)** – "to make cards." The verb 做 takes 卡片 as its object. Example from the story: 他们开始一起做卡片。
- **送礼物 (sòng lǐwù)** – "to give a gift." The verb 送 takes 礼物 as its object. Example from the story: 他们把礼物送给了老师。

### Resultative Complements

- **看到 (kàn dào)** – "to see." The complement 到 shows that the action of looking successfully achieved a result. Example from the story: 老师看到卡片上写的字，非常感动。
- **听到 (tīng dào)** – "to hear." The complement 到 shows that the action of listening achieved a result. Example from the story: 听到他们的话，老师开心地笑了。

### The 把 (bǎ) Sentence

- **把 + Object + Verb + 了** – This structure moves the object before the verb to emphasize what happens to it. Example from the story: 他们把礼物送给了老师。
