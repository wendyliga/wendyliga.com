## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases:

- 摘花 (zhāi huā) — "to pick flowers." The verb 摘 (to pick) takes 花 (flowers) directly as its object, a common verb + object pattern seen throughout the story.
- 做卡片 (zuò kǎpiàn) — "to make a card." The verb 做 combines with the object 卡片 to describe a complete action.
- 送礼物 (sòng lǐwù) — "to give a gift." 送 (to give as a present) plus 礼物 forms another useful everyday phrase.

### Resultative Complements:

- 写上 (xiě shang) — Here 上 works as a resultative complement, showing that the writing produced a result: the words are now on the card.
- 看到 (kàn dào) — 到 shows that the action of looking achieved its result, meaning "to see." Used in 老师一看到鲜花和卡片.
- 找来 (zhǎo lái) — 来 is a directional complement here, meaning the things were found and brought over.

### 一边...一边... (yìbiān... yìbiān...):

- 他们一边摘花，一边高兴地说着话。— This pattern expresses two actions happening at the same time: "They picked flowers while chatting happily."

### 一...就... (yī... jiù...):

- 老师一看到鲜花和卡片，就笑了。— This structure means "as soon as... then...": the moment the teacher saw the flowers and card, she smiled.

### 把 (bǎ) Sentence:

- 丽丽把花放在卡片旁边。— The 把 structure moves the object (花) before the verb to emphasize what is done with it.
- 他们把礼物送给了老师。— Same pattern, focusing on the gift being handed over.
