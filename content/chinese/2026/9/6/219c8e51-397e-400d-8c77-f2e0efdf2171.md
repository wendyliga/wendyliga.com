## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases (动宾短语)

The pattern is verb + object, very common in everyday Chinese:

- **穿衣服 (chuān yīfu)** — "to wear clothes" — 穿 + 衣服。In the story: 你们要多穿衣服。
- **捡叶子 (jiǎn yèzi)** — "to pick up leaves" — 捡 + 叶子。In the story: 我去捡叶子。
- **送礼物 (sòng lǐwù)** — "to give a gift" — 送 can take two objects: 送 + 老师 + 一个礼物。

### Resultative Complements (结果补语)

A complement after the verb shows the result of the action:

- **做好 (zuò hǎo)** — 好 shows the action is successfully completed: 他们一起做好了美丽的书签卡片。
- **回到 (huí dào)** — 到 shows the action reaches a place: 小明和丽丽回到了家。

### The 把 (bǎ) Construction

Pattern: Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + other element. It emphasizes what happens to the object:

- 小明和丽丽**把**卡片送给了老师。

### 像……一样 (xiàng… yīyàng)

Used to make similes, meaning "to be like…":

- 它们**像**小珍珠**一样**！
- 心里**像**吃了糖**一样**甜。

### 越来越 (yuè lái yuè)

Means "more and more," used before an adjective:

- 秋天**越来越**深了，天气也**越来越**凉。

### 因为……所以…… (yīnwèi… suǒyǐ…)

Means "because… therefore…":

- **因为**早上叶子上的露水是白色的，**所以**叫白露。
