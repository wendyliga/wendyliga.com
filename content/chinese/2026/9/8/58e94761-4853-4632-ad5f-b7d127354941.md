## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### Verb-Object Phrases:

- **做礼物 / 做卡片 (zuò lǐwù / zuò kǎpiàn)** — "to make a gift / make a card": the verb 做 directly takes an object, as in 想给老师做一个特别的礼物。
- **摘花 (zhāi huā)** — "to pick flowers": a simple verb + object structure, as in 他们去花园摘了一些新鲜的花。
- **送礼物 (sòng lǐwù)** — "to give a present": 送 takes 礼物 as its object, as in 把花和卡片一起送给了老师。

### The 把 (bǎ) Construction:

- **把花和卡片一起送给了老师** — Structure: Subject + 把 + Object + Verb + result (给 + person + 了). Used to describe what was done to a specific thing.

### Resultative Complements:

- **做好 (zuò hǎo)** — 好 as a resultative complement shows the action was completed successfully: 卡片做好以后 = "after the card was finished."
- **看到 (kàn dào)** — 到 shows the action achieved a result: 老师看到卡片上的字 = "the teacher saw (and noticed) the writing on the card."

### Other Useful Points:

- **一次又一次 (yí cì yòu yí cì)** — "again and again," a common pattern for repetition.
- **从那天起 (cóng nà tiān qǐ)** — "from that day on"; 从...起 marks the starting point of a period of time.
- **写得更好 (xiě de gèng hǎo)** — the complement 得 describes how an action is done, and 更 adds "even more": "to write even better."
