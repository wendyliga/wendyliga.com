## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### 就 (jiù) - "then / as early as"

- **教师节还有两天就到了。** — 就 connects a condition/time with a result, here meaning "only two days left and then it arrives."
- **他们很早就起床了。** — 很早 + 就 emphasizes that something happened earlier than expected.

### 决定 (juédìng) - "to decide"

- **他们决定给老师准备一份特别的礼物。** — 决定 + verb phrase; commonly followed by 给 + person + verb.

### 对...说 (duì...shuō) - "to say to someone"

- **小明对丽丽说：“……"** — 对 + person + 说 is the standard way to introduce dialogue.
- **老师对他们说：“……"** — same structure with a pronoun.

### 觉得 (juéde) - "to feel / to think"

- **他们觉得很骄傲，也很开心。** — 觉得 + adjective expresses feelings; 也 links two parallel adjectives.

### 因为...所以 (because...therefore)

- **因为他们想让老师更高兴。** — 因为 gives the reason; here 所以 is omitted, which is common in natural speech.

### 更 (gèng) - "even more"

- **学习更努力了 / 让老师更高兴。** — 更 + adjective compares to a previous state.
