## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### 1. 快……了 (kuài...le) — “about to”
This pattern shows that something is going to happen soon.  
- Example: 寒衣节快到了。  
- Meaning: The Winter Clothing Festival is about to arrive.

### 2. 把字句 (bǎ-sentence) — “disposal” construction
Used to show what is done to a specific object.  
- Example: 他们把纸衣和祭品放在桌子上。  
- Meaning: They put the paper clothes and offerings on the table.

### 3. 一边……一边…… (yìbiān... yìbiān...) — “while doing A, also doing B”
Used to describe two actions happening at the same time.  
- Example: 小明一边做一边说。  
- Meaning: Xiaoming talked while making paper clothes.

### 4. Verb-Object Phrases
These are common verb-object combinations in the story:  
- 送寒衣 (sòng hányī) — send winter clothes  
- 剪纸 (jiǎn zhǐ) — cut paper  
- 折衣服 (zhé yīfu) — fold clothes  
- 点香 (diǎn xiāng) — light incense  
- 鞠躬 (jū gōng) — bow

### 5. Resultative Complements
A result word after a verb shows the result of the action.  
- 做完 (zuò wán) — finish doing  
- 收到 (shōu dào) — receive successfully  
- 记得 (jì de) — remember  
- 走进 (zǒu jìn) — walk into
