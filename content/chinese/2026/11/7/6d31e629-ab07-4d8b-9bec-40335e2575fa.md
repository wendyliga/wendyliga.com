## Grammatical Points and Analysis

### 1. “把”字句  
- 奶奶把白菜洗干净。  
- 现在把罐子盖好。  
- Used to show how an object is handled or changed.

### 2. “先……再……” for sequence  
- 先在罐子里放一层盐，再放一层白菜。  
- It shows the order of actions.

### 3. “会” for possible results  
- 这样菜会出很多水。  
- 为什么酸菜会变酸？  
- It expresses that something will happen under certain conditions.

### 4. “因为……” for cause and result  
- 因为盐和白菜在一起，经过一些时间，菜就会变酸。  
- It connects a reason with a result.

### 5. Resultative complements  
- 洗干净  
- 切成两半  
- 盖好  
- 做好  
- 学会  
- These complements show the result of an action.
