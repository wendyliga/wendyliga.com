全家人围坐在桌旁，一边吃着甜甜的桃子，一边喝着热茶。妈妈笑着把桃酱抹在面包上分给大家。丽丽看着天上慢慢变黄的树叶，心里暖暖的。她知道，秋天来了，这是一个团圆和感恩的季节。

## English Translation

On the eve of the Start of Autumn, Lili and her younger brother Xiaoming were happily picking peaches in the yard. The peach tree in the yard was full of ripe peaches, red and soft.

Xiaoming said, “Sister, this peach is so big and red, it must be very sweet!”
Lili smiled and said, “I think it’s especially sweet too! Let’s pick more and have Mom teach us to make peach jam.”

They carried a full basket of peaches into the kitchen. Mom washed the peaches clean, removed the skins, and cut them into small pieces. Then she taught Lili and Xiaoming to put the peaches and sugar into a pot and simmer them over low heat. Xiaoming stirred carefully, and a sweet fragrance drifted out. Lili tasted a small bite and said happily, “It’s delicious! Much sweeter than store-bought jam.”

Dad set up a small table in the yard, placing the ancestors’ photos, an incense burner, and some fruit on it. He took Lili and Xiaoming to place the freshly made peach jam and the biggest peaches in front of the offerings. They lit incense and respectfully kowtowed three times. Dad said softly, “Autumn is coming. We ask our ancestors to bless our whole family with safety and a good harvest.” An evening breeze blew through the yard, bringing a touch of coolness.

The whole family sat around the table, eating sweet peaches and drinking hot tea. Mom smiled as she spread peach jam on bread and shared it with everyone. Lili looked at the leaves in the sky slowly turning yellow, and her heart felt warm. She knew that autumn had come—a season of reunion and gratitude.

- 祭祖 (jìzǔ) — worship ancestors (祭 = worship, 祖 = ancestors)
- 迎接秋天 (yíngjiē qiūtiān) — welcome autumn

### Resultative Complements:

- 熟透 (shú tòu) — ripe through; the complement 透 indicates thoroughness, meaning “completely ripe”
- 洗干净 (xǐ gānjìng) — wash clean; 干净 is the result of washing
- 切成 (qiē chéng) — cut into; 成 indicates the change of state into small pieces
- 做成 (zuò chéng) — make into; 成 signals successful transformation into jam
- 摆好 (bǎi hǎo) — arrange well; 好 shows that the action is completed and in a satisfactory state
